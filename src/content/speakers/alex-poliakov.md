    title: 'Building Crashproof Agents'
    abstract: |
      Beyond the problems of accuracy and cost, AI agents are challenged by
      long-running execution processes, human-in-the-loop approval logic,
      non-deterministic call paths, and upgrade complexity. Agents are expected
      to perform more important tasks over time, with increasing costs of
      failure. These challenges can be met with Durable Execution, a set of
      techniques that originated in transactional processing and is now growing
      in popularity with agent builders. This talk introduces durable execution
      and the guarantees it can, and cannot, provide, then shows why durability
      is becoming a key pattern in AI agents. It closes with how DBOS achieves
      durability using only Postgres, including advanced maneuvers like
      queueing, upgrading long-running workflows, and forking. For developers
      with experience in agentic development or an interest in reliable
      software. Postgres experience is a plus.
