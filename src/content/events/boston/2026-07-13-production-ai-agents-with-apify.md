A hands-on build night at Microsoft NERD with Apify, HackerSquad, and Builders
Collective. Kranthi Manchikanti opened the night and Petros Hong (Apify) gave
the featured talk, "Apify: Power to the Builders." Then the room built:
18 projects shipped in one evening, most wired to Apify actors and assembled
with tools like Bolt, Claude Code, and Supabase. Recordings and full build
details live on the
[HackerSquad event page](https://hackersquad.io/events/cmrfksvh002x6np0k3ogpgooi).

### What got built

- **TicketTracer**: tracks the real-time delta between face-value tickets and secondary-market markups across major touring events. [Repo](https://github.com/ankittejyadav/TicketTracer)
- **TruckCast**: shows the weather along a truck route using Apify's Google Maps Route and Weather scrapers. [Repo](https://github.com/FangQijun/msft_apify_event_app)
- **WealthWire**: turns the risk-factor section of every 10-K and 10-Q into structured, comparable data for screening the S&P 500. [Live](https://wealthwire.io/)
- **Domain Expiry Tracker**: paste a list of domain names and see which are expiring soon. [Demo](https://domain-expiry-tracke-mnag.bolt.host/)
- **Trends Radar**: a trends radar for social media. [Demo](https://bolt.new/p/68850439)
- **Configgo Lead Radar**: scores Boston's public development filings into prioritized real estate leads, flagging permits no one has covered yet. Built with Apify's RAG Web Browser and Bolt. [Demo](https://configgo-lead-radar-958x.bolt.host/)
- **Quick Question**: a party game where every player writes a question for a given answer, then the room votes for the funniest. [Demo](https://interactive-question-1w4w.bolt.host/)
- **HypeIndex**: scrapes what the press writes and plots it against what players actually do, to spot who is overhyped and who is flying under the radar. [Repo](https://github.com/HUmutI/hype-index)
- **NightFinds**: finds shops, restaurants, and services actually open right now, anywhere in the world, evaluated against each venue's local time. [Repo](https://github.com/Paramjeet-singh-neu/NightFinds)
- **InterviewForge**: interview prep across 16 tech companies, with 417 interview rounds and 1,143 practice questions. [Demo](https://faang-interview-prep-lzfe.bolt.host/)
- **The Multiverse Decider**: simulates branching future timelines from your life choices, grounded in live Apify search data. [Link](https://akhildaphara.github.io/)
- **Prospect Scout**: finds the first few customers for your project. [Repo](https://github.com/kkumar30/prospect-scout)
- **Congressional Trading Digest**: tracks trades by members of Congress.
- **Azure IaC Generator**: generates Azure infrastructure-as-code templates, parameter files, and deployment scripts, ready to run.
- **Situent**: an agent that helps you make sense of your time and how to do better. [Repo](https://github.com/sbel2/situent)
- **Pain Miner**: extracts complaints from Reddit posts, ranks them by intensity and volume, and surfaces business opportunities. [Demo](https://gifted-pain-miner-lab.base44.app/)
- **Job + HR Finder**: filters live-scraped AI/ML job postings by keywords from your resume, then finds the recruiters at each company. Built with Apify actors and Claude Code. [Repo](https://github.com/MuskanKhandelwal/job-post-HR-search)
- **GamePulse AI**: a real-time sports copilot that gives every live game an explainable 0 to 100 watchability score and tells fans what to watch right now. [Repo](https://github.com/sdileepkumarreddy)
