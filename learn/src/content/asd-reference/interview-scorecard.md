## Required checks

- [ ] Candidate states what the model may not do.
- [ ] At least one external effect has idempotency/reconciliation semantics.
- [ ] Untrusted content is separated from trusted instruction.
- [ ] Tenant/user identity reaches data and tools.
- [ ] Long-running or retry behavior is addressed when relevant.
- [ ] Evals include process/trajectory, not only final text.
- [ ] One SLO or quantitative capacity estimate is stated.
- [ ] Rollout/rollback is addressed.
- [ ] Two tradeoffs are tied to requirements.
