# Product decision log

This is the consolidated output of the product interview.

## Positioning

- Audience: aspiring FDEs.
- Primary outcome: pass Agentic System Design interviews.
- Product name: FDE Gym by The AI Runtime.
- Product role: free AIR learning tool.
- Home: Learn site.
- Primary moat: interview realism.

## Experience

- Chat plus structured architecture canvas.
- Structured blocks, custom labels, typed custom component.
- Optional technology label.
- Simple arrows with optional labels.
- Think aloud while designing.
- Candidate-led with selective Staff FDE intervention.
- Staff FDE persona also answers customer discovery questions.
- Canvas changes inform the next natural probe.
- Conversation only in V1, no customer artifacts.

## Modes

- Practice and Mock.
- 15-minute drill and 30-minute mock.
- Foundations, FDE, and Senior FDE bars.
- Adaptive difficulty inside the selected bar.
- Practice shows rubric, timer, references, hints, restart, and undo.
- Mock hides rubric and exact timer, with no hints or rewind.

## Interviewer behavior

- Calm and demanding.
- One meaningful follow-up for weak or vague answers.
- Devil's-advocate challenges for strong answers.
- Challenges technical errors without teaching in Mock mode.
- Allows clarification and distinguishes precision from confusion.
- Supports explicit assumptions and calibrated uncertainty.
- Curated customer knowledge boundaries.
- Adaptive pacing.
- Final architecture defense plus one executive challenge.
- Late constraint only for a strong candidate.
- Maintains structured coverage state, not a psychological candidate profile.

## Evaluation

- Hybrid deterministic and LLM evaluation.
- Separate interviewer and evaluator.
- Evaluator sees neutral timeline, not interviewer opinions.
- Evaluator sees meaningful graph revisions, not cosmetic movements.
- Independence weighting: independent, responsive, hinted, unresolved.
- Candidate omission separated from interviewer coverage.
- `INCOMPLETE ASSESSMENT` allowed.
- Verdict first, score second.
- Bar-relative result across Foundations, FDE, and Senior.
- Multiple defensible reference architectures.
- Technology-aware but vendor-neutral.
- One technical deep dive in a 30-minute mock.
- Lightweight quantitative reasoning.
- Lightweight build-versus-buy and rollout probes.
